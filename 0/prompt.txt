hey, so i'm building something for the dev hacktoberfest weekend challenge, "build for a friend". deadline is monday 5 oct, 12:29 pm ist.

my friend is job hunting and practising interviews alone doesn't really work. so i'm making interview room, a mock interviewer that actually talks to you. you speak, it listens, asks a follow up on what you said, and at the end gives you a proper report. it's for software engineers, with frontend, backend and ml tracks, and behavioural, technical and hr rounds.

everything runs in the browser, nothing gets uploaded. silero vad, whisper for hearing, gemma as the interviewer, kokoro for the voice, and a 3d avatar that lip syncs. it's built on react-ai-voice-avatar, which is my own npm package. this app is new though, started it this weekend.
